About Dr. Ekta

Dr. Ekta Dabaas is an evidence-driven physiotherapist with over 15 years of clinical experience. She combines manual therapy, tailored exercise programs and patient education to deliver measurable outcomes. She has worked with athletes, post-surgical patients and people with chronic pain.

Approach

Patient-centred, functional, and goal-oriented — we focus on what matters to your life and activity.

Techniques

Manual therapy, dry needling, kinesio taping, neuromuscular re-education and graded exercise.

Outcomes

Pain reduction, improved mobility, strength recovery, and a return to sport or daily activities.

Services

  • Musculoskeletal assessment & treatment
  • Post-operative rehabilitation
  • Sports injury prevention & rehab
  • Chronic pain management
  • Personalized home exercise programs
